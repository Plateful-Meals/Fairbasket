Mozzarella, tomato and pesto ciabatta

  • 10 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 ciabatta loaves
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 60g green pesto
  • 65g rocket

From your cupboard: black pepper.

You will need

A bread knife (and an oven or grill to warm the bread, if you like).

Method

  1. Slice each ciabatta in half lengthways, then cut each loaf in two to make four sandwiches.
  2. Spread the pesto over the bottom halves.
  3. Slice the mozzarella and tomatoes and layer them on top. Add the rocket and some black pepper.
  4. Put the tops on and press down gently.
  5. For a warm sandwich, heat the bread in an oven at 180°C (160°C fan) for 5 minutes before you fill it.
