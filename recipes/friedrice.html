Egg fried rice

  • 25 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 300g long grain rice
  • 4 free-range eggs
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 30ml soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A saucepan and a wok or large frying pan.

Method

  1. Cook the rice in boiling water for 10 to 12 minutes, drain and spread it out to steam dry. Leftover rice works even better, as long as it was cooled quickly, kept in the fridge and is used within a day.
  2. Slice the spring onions, chop the garlic and beat the eggs in a bowl.
  3. Heat a splash of oil in a wok over a high heat. Add the garlic and peas and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Push everything to one side, pour the eggs into the space and stir until just set.
  5. Add the rice, soy sauce and spring onions and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until piping hot.
