BBQ pulled chicken buns

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 600g chicken breast fillets
  • 200g bBQ sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 4 burger buns
  • 300g coleslaw

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A slow cooker (or a saucepan with a lid) and two forks.

Method

  1. Slice the onion and put it in the slow cooker with the chicken breasts.
  2. Pour over the BBQ sauce and 100ml of water, and turn the chicken to coat it.
  3. Cook on low for 4 to 5 hours, or on high for 2 and a half to 3 hours, until the chicken is cooked through and pulls apart easily.
  4. Lift the chicken out, shred it with two forks and stir it back into the sauce.
  5. Pile it into the buns with the coleslaw.
  6. No slow cooker? Simmer it in a covered saucepan over a low heat for 25 to 30 minutes, until the chicken has no pink in the middle, then shred it.
