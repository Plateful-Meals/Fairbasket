Peanut noodles

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Compare prices for this recipe

Ingredients

  • 250g medium egg noodles
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 45ml soy sauce
  • 45ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 300g stir-fry vegetables
  • 1 bunch spring onions

You will need

A saucepan, a colander and a bowl.

Method

  1. In a bowl, stir the peanut butter, soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce with 4 tablespoons of hot water until smooth.
  2. Cook the noodles in a saucepan of boiling water for 4 minutes (check the packet).
  3. Add the stir fry veg to the pan for the last 2 minutes, so it keeps some crunch.
  4. Drain, tip everything back into the pan and stir in the peanut sauce.
  5. Slice the spring onions and scatter them over the top. This is good cold the next day too.
