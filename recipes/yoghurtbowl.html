Yoghurt, granola and berries

  • 5 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 600g natural yoghurt
  • 200g honey granola
  • 200g frozen mixed berries
  • 40g runny honey

You will need

A bowl for each person.

Method

  1. Take the berries out of the freezer first. They soften in about 10 minutes, or you can microwave them for 30 seconds.
  2. Spoon the yoghurt into four bowls.
  3. Top with the granola and berries, then drizzle with honey.
  4. Eat it straight away so the granola stays crunchy.
