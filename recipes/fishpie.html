Fish pie

  • 60 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 640g fish pie mix
  • 1kg white potatoes
  • 500ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 50g salted butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 80g mature cheddar

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

Two saucepans, a whisk, a masher and an ovenproof dish.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Peel the potatoes, cut them into chunks and boil for 15 to 20 minutes, until soft.
  2. Melt 30g of the butter in a second saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in 450ml of the milk a little at a time, then simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until thick.
  3. Stir the fish and the peas into the sauce and season. Tip it into an ovenproof dish.
  4. Drain the potatoes and mash them with the rest of the butter and milk.
  5. Spread the mash over the fish, rough it up with a fork and scatter over the grated cheese.
  6. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until golden and bubbling at the edges. The fish should be white and flake easily all the way through.
