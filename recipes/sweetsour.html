Sweet and sour chicken

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 10 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast fillets
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 green pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 432g pineapple chunks in juice
  • 60g tomato ketchup
  • 30ml soy sauce
  • 30ml white wine vinegar
  • 15g cornflour
  • 300g long grain rice

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A wok or large frying pan, a saucepan and a small bowl.

Method

  1. Start the rice in a saucepan of boiling water. It takes 10 to 12 minutes (check the packet).
  2. Cut the chicken, peppers and onion into bite-sized pieces. Drain the pineapple, keeping the juice.
  3. In a small bowl, mix the ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar and cornflour with 6 tablespoons of the pineapple juice.
  4. Stir-fry the chicken in a little oil over a high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, until golden and cooked through with no pink in the middle.
  5. Add the peppers and onion for 3 minutes, then the pineapple and the sauce. Stir for 2 minutes, until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
  6. Drain the rice and serve the chicken on top.
