Sweet potato jackets with chilli beans

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1kg sweet potatoes
  • 400g mixed beans in water
  • 400g chopped tomatoes
  • 3g chilli powder
  • 150g soured cream

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A microwave (or an oven) and a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl.

Method

  1. Scrub the sweet potatoes and prick them all over with a fork. Pick four that are about the same size.
  2. Microwave two at a time on high for 8 to 10 minutes, turning them halfway, until soft all the way through when squeezed.
  3. Meanwhile, drain the beans and simmer them in a saucepan with the chopped tomatoes and chilli powder for 10 minutes, until thick. Season. A microwave works too: 5 minutes in a covered bowl, stirred halfway.
  4. Split the potatoes open, spoon in the beans and top with soured cream.
  5. In an oven, bake the potatoes at 200°C (180°C fan) for 45 to 50 minutes instead.
