Garlic and chilli prawn linguine

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 400g linguine
  • 300g raw king prawns
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 fresh red chilli
  • 1 lemon
  • 15g fresh parsley

From your cupboard: olive oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A saucepan and a large frying pan.

Method

  1. Cook the linguine in boiling salted water for 9 to 11 minutes (check the packet). Keep a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
  2. Finely chop the garlic, chilli and parsley. Take the seeds out of the chilli if you want it milder.
  3. Heat a good splash of olive oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Cook the garlic and chilli for 1 minute, without letting them brown.
  4. Add the prawns and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until they are pink all over.
  5. Add the pasta, the juice of the lemon, the parsley and a splash of the cooking water. Toss for 1 minute and season.
