Chicken stir fry

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast fillets
  • 250g medium egg noodles
  • 600g stir-fry vegetables
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 45ml soy sauce

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A wok or large frying pan and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Slice the chicken into thin strips, slice the pepper and chop the garlic.
  2. Cook the noodles in boiling water (usually 4 minutes, check the packet). Drain and toss with a drop of oil so they don't stick.
  3. Heat a splash of oil in a wok over a high heat. Stir-fry the chicken for 5 to 6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  4. Add the pepper, stir-fry veg and garlic and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes, so the veg still has some crunch.
  5. Add the noodles and soy sauce and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until everything is hot. Serve straight away.
