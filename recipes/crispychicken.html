Crispy paprika chicken and chips

  • 35 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 800g oven chips
  • 6g paprika
  • 300g coleslaw

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An air fryer (or an oven and a baking tray).

Method

  1. Toss the chicken thighs with the paprika, a little oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Air fryer: cook the chicken at 190°C for 16 to 20 minutes, turning halfway. It is done when the thickest piece has no pink in the middle and the juices run clear. Put it on a plate and cover it to keep warm.
  3. Cook the chips in the air fryer at 200°C for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket twice (check the packet).
  4. Oven: heat it to 200°C (180°C fan). Spread the chicken and the chips on a baking tray, or two if they are crowded, and cook for 25 to 30 minutes, turning everything halfway. Check the chicken the same way.
  5. Serve with the coleslaw.
