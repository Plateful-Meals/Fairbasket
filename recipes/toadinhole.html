Toad in the hole

  • 50 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages
  • 140g plain flour
  • 4 free-range eggs
  • 200ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 30g gravy granules
  • 300g frozen peas

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven, a roasting tin or ovenproof dish, a jug and a small saucepan.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Whisk the flour, eggs and milk with 100ml of water and a pinch of salt until smooth. Leave the batter to stand.
  2. Put the sausages in a roasting tin or ovenproof dish with 2 tablespoons of oil. Cook in the oven for 10 minutes, until starting to brown.
  3. Take the tin out, quickly pour the batter around the sausages and put it straight back in.
  4. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the batter is puffed up and golden. Do not open the oven door for the first 20 minutes, or it sinks.
  5. Boil the peas for 3 minutes. Make the gravy with the granules and boiling water, following the tub.
  6. Check a sausage is cooked through with no pink, then serve with the peas and gravy.
