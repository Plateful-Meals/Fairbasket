Smoked salmon and soft cheese bagels

  • 10 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients

Compare prices for this recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 bagels
  • 180g soft cheese
  • 120g smoked salmon
  • 1 lemon

From your cupboard: black pepper.

You will need

A knife (and a toaster, if you like them toasted).

Method

  1. Slice the bagels in half. Toast them if you like.
  2. Spread the soft cheese over the bottom halves.
  3. Lay the smoked salmon on top and squeeze over a little lemon juice.
  4. Add black pepper and put the tops on.
