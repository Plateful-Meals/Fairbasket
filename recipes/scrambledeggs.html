Scrambled eggs on toast

  • 10 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 8 free-range eggs
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 30g salted butter
  • 60ml semi-skimmed milk

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A saucepan or frying pan (or a microwave) and a toaster.

Method

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the milk and a pinch of salt and pepper, and beat with a fork.
  2. Put the bread in the toaster.
  3. Melt half the butter in a pan over a low heat. Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a wooden spoon for 3 to 4 minutes, until they are softly set with no runny liquid left.
  4. Take the pan off the heat straight away, as the eggs keep cooking in the hot pan.
  5. Butter the toast and pile the eggs on top.
  6. No hob? Microwave the beaten eggs in a bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring between each, for about 2 minutes in total.
