Prawn stir-fry noodles

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 300g cooked king prawns
  • 250g rice noodles
  • 600g stir-fry vegetables
  • 45ml soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 20g fresh ginger
  • 60ml sweet chilli sauce

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A wok or large frying pan and a bowl or saucepan for the noodles.

Method

  1. Put the rice noodles in a bowl or saucepan, cover them with boiling water and leave for 3 to 5 minutes until soft (check the packet). Drain them.
  2. Finely chop the garlic and ginger.
  3. Heat a little oil in a wok or large frying pan over a high heat. Stir-fry the veg for 3 minutes, then add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
  4. Add the prawns. Cooked prawns need 2 minutes to heat through. Raw prawns need 3 to 4 minutes, until they are pink all over.
  5. Add the noodles, soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce, and toss everything together for 1 minute until hot.
