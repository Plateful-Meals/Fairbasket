Potato waffles, bacon and egg

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 3 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 potato waffles
  • 8 rashers smoked back bacon
  • 4 free-range eggs

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A toaster (or a grill, oven or air fryer) and a frying pan.

Method

  1. Cook the potato waffles from frozen. In a toaster they take two rounds on a medium setting. Under a grill or in an oven at 220°C (200°C fan) they take 10 to 12 minutes, turned once. In an air fryer they take 8 minutes at 200°C.
  2. Fry the bacon over a medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, then lift it onto a plate.
  3. Fry the eggs in the same pan for 2 to 3 minutes, until the whites are set.
  4. Put two waffles on each plate with the bacon and an egg on top.
