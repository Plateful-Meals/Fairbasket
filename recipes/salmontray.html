Salmon with new potatoes and broccoli

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 350g broccoli
  • 1 lemon
  • 30g salted butter

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven and a baking tray (or an air fryer) and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve any large potatoes and boil them in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes, until tender.
  2. Put the salmon on a baking tray, rub it with a little oil and season it. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until it flakes easily and is the same pale pink all the way through.
  3. Cut the broccoli into florets and add it to the potato pan for the last 4 minutes.
  4. Drain the vegetables and toss them with the butter.
  5. Serve the salmon with the vegetables and a wedge of lemon.
  6. In an air fryer the salmon takes 8 to 10 minutes at 180°C.
