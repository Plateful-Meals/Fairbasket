Loaded nachos

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 300g tortilla chips
  • 200g mature cheddar
  • 400g black beans
  • 200g tomato salsa
  • 60g sliced jalapeños

You will need

An oven and an ovenproof dish or baking tray.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Drain and rinse the beans and grate the cheese.
  2. Spread half the tortilla chips over an ovenproof dish or tray, then scatter over half the beans and cheese.
  3. Repeat with the rest of the chips, beans and cheese, so there's cheese in every layer.
  4. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until the cheese has melted.
  5. Spoon over the salsa and scatter over the jalapeños. Eat straight away, before the chips go soft.
