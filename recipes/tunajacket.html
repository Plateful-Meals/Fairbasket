Jacket potatoes with tuna mayo

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 baking potatoes
  • 290g tuna chunks in spring water
  • 80ml mayonnaise
  • 198g sweetcorn
  • 20g salted butter

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A microwave (or an oven, or an air fryer) and a bowl.

Method

  1. Scrub the potatoes and prick each one several times with a fork.
  2. Microwave two at a time on high for about 10 minutes, turning them over halfway, until they feel soft all the way through when squeezed. Leave them to stand for 2 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, drain the tuna and sweetcorn and mix them with the mayonnaise and a little salt and pepper.
  4. Cut a cross in each potato, squeeze it open, add a little butter and pile on the tuna.
  5. For a crisp skin, bake them in an oven at 200°C (180°C fan) for about 1 hour 15 minutes, or in an air fryer at 200°C for 40 to 45 minutes.
