Chilli con carne

  • 40 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 8 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 400g red kidney beans
  • 800g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 red pepper
  • 6g chilli powder
  • 300g long grain rice

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large pan and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Chop the onion, garlic and pepper.
  2. Heat a splash of oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion and pepper for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and chilli powder for 1 minute.
  3. Add the mince, turn the heat up and cook for 5 to 8 minutes until browned with no pink left.
  4. Drain the kidney beans and add them with the chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick.
  5. While it simmers, cook the rice in boiling water for 10 to 12 minutes (check the packet), then drain.
  6. Season the chilli and serve with the rice.
