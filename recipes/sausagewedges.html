Sausages, wedges and beans

  • 45 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages
  • 1kg white potatoes
  • 820g baked beans
  • 4g paprika

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven and a baking tray (or an air fryer) and a saucepan or a microwave.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the potatoes into wedges, leaving the skins on.
  2. Toss the wedges on a baking tray with a little oil, the paprika, salt and pepper. Bake for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the sausages to the tray and bake for 25 minutes more, turning everything halfway. The sausages are done when they are browned all over with no pink in the middle.
  4. Heat the beans in a saucepan over a low heat for 3 to 4 minutes, or in the microwave in a covered bowl for 3 minutes, stirred halfway.
  5. In an air fryer, cook the wedges at 200°C for 20 minutes, shaking twice. Keep them warm, then cook the sausages at 180°C for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once.
