Coronation chicken sandwiches

  • 25 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast fillets
  • 80ml mayonnaise
  • 4g curry powder
  • 40g mango chutney
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 120g mixed salad leaves

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A saucepan, a bowl and two forks.

Method

  1. Put the chicken in a saucepan, cover it with cold water and bring to the boil. Turn the heat down and simmer for 12 to 15 minutes, until cooked through with no pink in the middle.
  2. Lift the chicken out and let it cool for 5 minutes, then shred it with two forks.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, curry powder and mango chutney in a bowl. Stir in the chicken and season.
  4. Pile onto four slices of bread, add some salad and top with four more slices.
  5. The filling keeps in the fridge for 2 days, so it works for packed lunches.
