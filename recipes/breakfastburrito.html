Breakfast burritos

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 plain tortilla wraps
  • 6 free-range eggs
  • 100g mature cheddar
  • 410g baked beans
  • 8 frozen hash browns
  • 15g salted butter

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven, a baking tray, a small saucepan and a frying pan.

Method

  1. Cook the hash browns in the oven following the pack (usually about 20 minutes), turning halfway.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the beans in a small saucepan and grate the cheese.
  3. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper. Melt the butter in a frying pan over a low heat, add the eggs and stir gently for 2 to 3 minutes until just set.
  4. Warm the wraps, then fill each with egg, beans, two hash browns and cheese.
  5. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly. To freeze, cool them, wrap each one in foil and freeze. Reheat from frozen in the oven for 25 to 30 minutes until piping hot all the way through.
