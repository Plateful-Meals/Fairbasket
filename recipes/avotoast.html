Avocado and egg on toast

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 avocados
  • 4 free-range eggs
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 1 lemon
  • 2g chilli flakes

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A frying pan, a bowl, a fork and a toaster.

Method

  1. Halve the avocados, take out the stones and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash it with a fork, a squeeze of lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  2. Toast four slices of bread.
  3. Fry the eggs in a little oil over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until the whites are set.
  4. Spread the avocado on the toast and put an egg on each slice.
  5. Finish with chilli flakes and black pepper.
