Bean and cheese quesadillas

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 8 plain tortilla wraps
  • 400g red kidney beans
  • 198g sweetcorn
  • 200g mature cheddar
  • 1 bunch spring onions

You will need

A frying pan and a fork.

Method

  1. Drain and rinse the beans and sweetcorn. Roughly mash the beans with a fork.
  2. Grate the cheese and slice the spring onions.
  3. Spread some beans over half of a wrap, scatter over sweetcorn, spring onion and cheese, then fold the wrap in half.
  4. Cook in a dry frying pan over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden and the cheese has melted.
  5. Cut into triangles and repeat with the rest of the wraps.
