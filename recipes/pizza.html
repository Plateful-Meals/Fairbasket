Pepperoni pizza

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 pizza bases
  • 200g passata
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 90g pepperoni slices
  • 2g Mixed Italian herbs
  • 120g mixed salad leaves

You will need

An oven and a baking tray (or an air fryer).

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan).
  2. Spread the passata over the two pizza bases, leaving a finger-width edge.
  3. Tear the mozzarella over the top, add the pepperoni and sprinkle with the herbs.
  4. Bake on a baking tray for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and the edges are crisp.
  5. Cut into slices and serve half a pizza each with the salad.
  6. In an air fryer, cook one pizza at a time at 180°C for 6 to 8 minutes. Check it fits in the basket first.
