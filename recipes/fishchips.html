Fish, chips and peas

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 breaded fish fillets
  • 800g oven chips
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 1 lemon

From your cupboard: salt and vinegar.

You will need

An oven and a baking tray (or an air fryer) and a small saucepan or a microwave.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan).
  2. Spread the chips and the fish on a baking tray, or two if they are crowded. Bake from frozen for 20 to 25 minutes, turning the chips halfway. Packets differ, so check both.
  3. The fish is done when it is piping hot in the middle and the coating is crisp and golden.
  4. Boil the peas for 3 minutes and drain them, or microwave them in a covered bowl with a splash of water for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges, salt and vinegar.
  6. In an air fryer, cook the chips at 200°C for 12 to 15 minutes, then the fish at 190°C for 12 to 14 minutes.
