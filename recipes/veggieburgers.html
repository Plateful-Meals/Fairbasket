Veggie burgers and sweet potato wedges

  • 35 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 vegetarian burgers
  • 4 burger buns
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 40ml mayonnaise

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven and a baking tray (or an air fryer).

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the sweet potatoes into wedges, leaving the skins on, and toss them on a baking tray with a little oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Bake the wedges for 15 minutes.
  3. Turn the wedges, add the burgers to the tray and cook for 15 to 18 minutes more, turning the burgers halfway (check the packet).
  4. Split the buns. Slice the tomatoes and separate the lettuce leaves.
  5. Build the burgers with mayonnaise, lettuce and tomato, and serve with the wedges.
  6. In an air fryer, cook the wedges at 200°C for 18 minutes, shaking twice, then the burgers at 180°C for 10 minutes.
