Slow cooker beef stew

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g diced beef casserole steak
  • 400g carrots
  • 800g white potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 2 beef stock pots
  • 30g tomato purée
  • 30g plain flour

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A slow cooker (or a large pan with a lid, or a covered ovenproof dish).

Method

  1. Peel the carrots and potatoes and cut them into chunks. Chop the onions.
  2. Toss the beef in the flour with a little salt and pepper, so each piece is coated. The flour thickens the gravy.
  3. Put everything into the slow cooker. Stir the stock pots and tomato purée into 600ml of boiling water and pour it over.
  4. Put the lid on and cook on low for 8 hours, or on high for 4 to 5 hours, until the beef falls apart when you press it.
  5. Stir, taste and season before serving.
  6. No slow cooker? Simmer it gently in a large covered pan on the hob for 2 to 2 and a half hours, stirring now and then, or cook it in a covered dish in the oven at 160°C (140°C fan) for about 3 hours. Add a splash of water if it gets dry.
