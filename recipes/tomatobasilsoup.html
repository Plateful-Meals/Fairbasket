Tomato and basil soup

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 800g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 vegetable stock cube
  • 30g fresh basil
  • 100ml single cream
  • 1 loaf sliced bread

From your cupboard: oil, salt, pepper and a pinch of sugar.

You will need

A large saucepan and a blender.

Method

  1. Chop the onion and garlic. Heat a little oil in a large saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes, crumble in the stock cube and pour in 400ml of boiling water. Add a pinch of sugar, which takes the sharpness off tinned tomatoes.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add most of the basil and blend until smooth. Let it cool for a few minutes first, and never fill a blender more than halfway with hot soup.
  5. Stir in the cream, season, and warm it through without boiling. Top with the rest of the basil and serve with the bread.
