Rice and bean stuffed peppers

  • 35 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 red peppers
  • 250g microwave basmati rice
  • 400g black beans
  • 198g sweetcorn
  • 100g mature cheddar
  • 150g tomato salsa

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

An oven and a baking tray or ovenproof dish, a bowl and a microwave for the rice.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the peppers in half through the stalk and take out the seeds.
  2. Put the halves cut-side up in an ovenproof dish, rub them with a little oil and bake for 10 minutes.
  3. Heat the rice following the packet. Mix it in a bowl with the drained beans, the drained sweetcorn, the salsa and half the grated cheese. Season.
  4. Spoon the filling into the peppers and scatter over the rest of the cheese.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes, until the peppers are soft and the cheese is golden.
  6. No microwave? Tip the rice into a pan with 2 tablespoons of water and warm it over a low heat for 3 minutes.
