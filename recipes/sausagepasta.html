Sausage pasta

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 6 pork sausages
  • 400g penne pasta
  • 800g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2g chilli powder
  • 60g mature cheddar

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large frying pan and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins and roll it into small balls (or just slice the sausages).
  2. Fry in a splash of oil over a medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  3. Add the chopped onion and garlic and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and chilli powder and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the penne in boiling salted water for 10 to 12 minutes. Drain, stir into the sauce and serve with grated cheese.
