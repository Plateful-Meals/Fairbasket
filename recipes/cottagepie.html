Cottage pie

  • 60 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 8 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 300g carrots
  • 1kg white potatoes
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 30g tomato purée
  • 30g salted butter
  • 60ml semi-skimmed milk

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large pan, a saucepan, a masher and an ovenproof dish.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Peel the potatoes, cut into chunks and boil in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes until soft.
  2. Meanwhile, finely chop the onion and carrots and fry in a splash of oil over a medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes.
  3. Add the mince, turn the heat up and brown for 5 to 8 minutes until there's no pink left.
  4. Dissolve the stock cube in about 100ml of boiling water per person and add it with the tomato purée. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Drain the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk.
  6. Spoon the mince into an ovenproof dish, spread the mash on top and rough it up with a fork. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until golden.
  7. It freezes really well. Cool leftovers quickly, then freeze in portions for up to 3 months and reheat until piping hot.
