Spaghetti Bolognese

  • 35 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 8 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 400g chopped tomatoes
  • 30g tomato purée
  • 2g Mixed Italian herbs
  • 40g Parmigiano Reggiano

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large frying pan and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat a splash of oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Turn the heat up, add the mince and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 5 to 8 minutes until browned all over with no pink left.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée and herbs. Season, then let it bubble gently for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in a big pan of boiling salted water for 10 to 12 minutes (check the packet), then drain.
  6. Serve the sauce on the spaghetti with grated parmesan on top.
  7. Made a big batch? Cool the leftover sauce quickly, then keep it in the fridge for up to 2 days or freeze it in portions for up to 3 months.
