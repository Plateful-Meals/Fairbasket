Beef and bean burritos

  • 35 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 plain tortilla wraps
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 400g red kidney beans
  • 200g tomato salsa
  • 100g mature cheddar
  • 30g fajita seasoning

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A large frying pan and a saucepan.

Method

  1. Cook the rice in a saucepan of boiling water for 10 to 12 minutes (check the packet), then drain.
  2. Fry the mince in a little oil over a high heat for 5 to 8 minutes, breaking it up, until browned with no pink left.
  3. Stir in the fajita seasoning, the drained beans and 100ml of water. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Grate the cheese. Warm the tortillas for a few seconds each in a dry pan or the microwave so they fold without cracking.
  5. Put rice, mince, salsa and cheese along the middle of each tortilla. Fold in the two sides, then roll up from the bottom.
  6. It makes eight, so two each. Wrapped in foil they keep in the fridge for the next day.
