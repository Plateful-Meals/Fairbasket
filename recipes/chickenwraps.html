Chicken salad wraps

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast fillets
  • 4 plain tortilla wraps
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 60ml mayonnaise
  • 4g paprika

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A frying pan (or an air fryer).

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into thin strips and toss them with the paprika and a little salt and pepper.
  2. Fry in a little oil over a medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through with no pink in the middle.
  3. Shred the lettuce and slice the tomatoes.
  4. Spread mayonnaise over each wrap, then add the chicken, lettuce and tomato.
  5. Fold in the bottom, roll up tightly and cut in half.
  6. In an air fryer the chicken strips take 8 to 10 minutes at 190°C, shaken halfway.
