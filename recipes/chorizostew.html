Chorizo and butter bean stew

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 7 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 150g chorizo
  • 800g butter beans
  • 800g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 4g smoked paprika
  • 1 loaf sliced bread

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A large pan.

Method

  1. Slice the chorizo. Chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Fry the chorizo in a large pan over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until its red oil runs out. You will not need any other oil.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and smoked paprika for 1 minute.
  4. Tip in the chopped tomatoes and the drained butter beans. Simmer for 15 minutes, until the sauce has thickened.
  5. Season and serve in bowls with the bread to mop up the sauce.
