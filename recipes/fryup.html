Full English breakfast

  • 30 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 8 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers smoked back bacon
  • 4 free-range eggs
  • 410g baked beans
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 20g salted butter

From your cupboard: oil.

You will need

A large frying pan, a small saucepan and a toaster.

Method

  1. Fry the sausages in a little oil over a medium heat for 15 to 20 minutes, turning often, until browned all over and cooked through with no pink in the middle.
  2. Halve the tomatoes and slice the mushrooms. Push the sausages to one side and add the bacon, tomatoes and mushrooms to the pan. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes, turning the bacon once.
  3. Heat the beans in a small saucepan over a low heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Move everything onto warm plates, then fry the eggs in the same pan for 2 to 3 minutes until the whites are set.
  5. Toast and butter the bread and serve it all together.
  6. Got a grill or an air fryer? The sausages and bacon can go in there instead, which frees up the pan.
