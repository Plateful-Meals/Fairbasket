Bacon sandwiches

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 4 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 rashers smoked back bacon
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 30g salted butter
  • 40g tomato ketchup

You will need

A frying pan (or a grill, or an air fryer).

Method

  1. Fry the bacon over a medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until cooked and as crisp as you like it. No oil is needed, as the bacon has its own fat.
  2. Butter the bread. You need two slices per sandwich.
  3. Put two rashers in each sandwich, add ketchup and cut in half.
  4. Under a grill the bacon takes 3 to 4 minutes a side. In an air fryer it takes 6 to 8 minutes at 200°C, turned once.
