Shepherd's pie

  • 65 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 9 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 onion
  • 300g carrots
  • 1kg white potatoes
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 30g tomato purée
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 30g salted butter
  • 60ml semi-skimmed milk

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large pan, a saucepan, a masher and an ovenproof dish.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Peel the potatoes, cut them into chunks and boil for 15 to 20 minutes, until soft.
  2. Chop the onion and dice the carrots. Fry them in a little oil in a large pan for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the lamb mince and cook for 5 to 8 minutes, breaking it up, until browned with no pink left.
  4. Stir in the tomato purée, crumble in the stock cube and add 300ml of boiling water. Simmer for 15 minutes, then stir in the peas.
  5. Drain the potatoes and mash them with the butter and milk. Season.
  6. Spoon the lamb into an ovenproof dish, spread the mash on top and rough it up with a fork. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden.
