Sweetcorn fritters

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 396g sweetcorn
  • 120g self-raising flour
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 100ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 60ml sweet chilli sauce

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A frying pan, a mixing bowl and a spatula.

Method

  1. Drain the sweetcorn well. Slice the spring onions.
  2. Beat the eggs and milk in a mixing bowl, then stir in the flour to make a thick batter. Season it.
  3. Stir in the sweetcorn and spring onions.
  4. Heat a thin layer of oil in a frying pan over a medium heat. Drop in heaped tablespoons of the mixture and flatten them slightly.
  5. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden and cooked through. Cook them in batches. It makes about 12.
  6. Serve with sweet chilli sauce for dipping.
