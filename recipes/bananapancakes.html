Fluffy banana pancakes

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 250ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 3 bananas
  • 30g salted butter
  • 60g runny honey

You will need

A frying pan, a mixing bowl, a fork and a spatula.

Method

  1. Mash one banana in a mixing bowl with a fork. Beat in the eggs and milk.
  2. Add the flour and stir until just mixed. A few small lumps are fine, and they make the pancakes fluffier.
  3. Melt a little butter in a frying pan over a medium heat. Drop in 2 tablespoons of batter for each pancake, with space between them.
  4. Cook for about 2 minutes, until bubbles show on top, then flip and cook for 1 minute more. Keep going until the batter is used up. It makes about 12.
  5. Stack them up, top with the other two bananas, sliced, and drizzle with honey.
