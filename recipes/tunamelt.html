Tuna melts

  • 15 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 5 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • 290g tuna chunks in spring water
  • 150g mature cheddar
  • 60ml mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch spring onions

From your cupboard: black pepper.

You will need

A grill and a baking tray (or a frying pan, or an air fryer).

Method

  1. Heat the grill to high. Drain the tuna, grate the cheese and slice the spring onions.
  2. Mix the tuna, mayonnaise, spring onions and half the cheese with some black pepper.
  3. Toast four slices of bread on one side under the grill.
  4. Turn them over, spread the tuna mixture on the untoasted side and scatter over the rest of the cheese.
  5. Grill for 3 to 4 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
  6. No grill? Make them as closed sandwiches and fry them in a dry pan for 3 minutes a side, or cook the open ones in an air fryer at 190°C for 5 minutes.
