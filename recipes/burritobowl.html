Burrito bowls

  • 25 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g black beans
  • 198g sweetcorn
  • 150g tomato salsa
  • 100g mature cheddar
  • 1 red pepper

From your cupboard: salt and pepper.

You will need

A saucepan and a small pan.

Method

  1. Cook the rice in boiling water for 10 to 12 minutes, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, drain and rinse the black beans and sweetcorn. Warm them in a small pan with a splash of water for 3 to 4 minutes, and season.
  3. Dice the pepper and grate the cheese.
  4. Divide the rice between bowls and arrange the beans, sweetcorn, pepper, salsa and cheese on top.
  5. Got leftover cooked chicken or mince? Heat it until piping hot and add it too.
