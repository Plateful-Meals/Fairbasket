Prawn cocktail wraps

  • 10 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 6 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 300g cooked peeled prawns
  • 60ml mayonnaise
  • 20g tomato ketchup
  • 2 little gem lettuces
  • 4 plain tortilla wraps
  • 1 lemon

From your cupboard: black pepper.

You will need

A bowl.

Method

  1. Mix the mayonnaise and ketchup in a bowl with a squeeze of lemon juice and some black pepper.
  2. Drain the prawns, pat them dry with kitchen paper and stir them into the sauce.
  3. Shred the lettuce.
  4. Divide the lettuce and prawns between the wraps, fold in the bottom and roll up tightly.
  5. Keep them cold and eat them the same day.
