Beef lasagne

  • 75 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • 10 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g lasagne sheets
  • 800g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2g Mixed Italian herbs
  • 500ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 40g salted butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 120g mature cheddar

From your cupboard: oil, salt and pepper.

You will need

A large pan, a saucepan, a whisk and an ovenproof dish.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Fry the onion in a little oil in a large pan for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and mince. Cook for 5 to 8 minutes, breaking the mince up, until browned with no pink left.
  3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and herbs, season, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. For the white sauce, melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the milk a little at a time, then simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until thick. Stir in half the cheese.
  5. Layer it up in an ovenproof dish: a third of the meat sauce, a layer of pasta sheets, a little white sauce. Do that twice more and finish with the rest of the white sauce and cheese.
  6. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until golden, bubbling and the pasta is soft when you push a knife in. Leave it for 10 minutes before cutting.
  7. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 days and freeze well in portions.
